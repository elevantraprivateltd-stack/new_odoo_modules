Expense breakdown lines & enhanced recurring payment templates for Odoo 19 Community Edition

Module Overview

The Recurring Extend module enhances Odoo's accounting capabilities by introducing line-item expense breakdowns directly into recurring payment templates. Designed to work smoothly with base_accounting_kit, it allows finance teams to automate detailed recurring journal entries and expense distribution accurately.

Key Features

Streamline recurring payments and automate expense allocations.

Expense Breakdown Lines

Add granular expense breakdown lines to recurring templates to allocate costs across multiple accounts or sub-categories automatically.

Recurring Templates Views

Customized template views that simplify creating, editing, and managing automated recurring vendor bills, payments, and entries.

Accounting Kit Extension

Seamlessly extends `base_accounting_kit` functionality, ensuring strict security access rules (`ir.model.access.csv`) and reliable execution.

Automated Financial Entries

Eliminates manual entry errors by automatically populating line breakdowns whenever a recurring transaction is processed.
