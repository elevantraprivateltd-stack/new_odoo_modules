Module Overview

The Lead Custom Labels module modifies standard Odoo CRM and Partner views to align perfectly with institutional and client-focused workflows. It customizes tab names, updates field terminology, and adds dedicated resource assignment capabilities.

Key Features & Changes

Tailored updates for CRM Forms and Partner records.

CRM Label Adjustments

Renamed "Expected Closing" to "Lead End Date" on the CRM form for better timeline tracking.

Custom Tabs Setup

Updated tab layout with dedicated "Lead" and "Client Info" tabs (replacing default Contact/Partner tabs).

Resource Management

Added a custom "Task Assigned to Resource" field in CRM to easily track team assignments.

Institute terminology

Customized partner/school terminology by renaming "Company" to "Institute".
