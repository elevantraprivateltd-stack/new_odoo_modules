FOL. 01  —  OVERVIEW

A lighter relabeling layer for teams whose CRM vocabulary isn't "Company" and "Expected Closing."

Lead Custom Labels doesn't add a new model or a new workflow — it reshapes the CRM and Partner views you already use. Field names, tab names, and one new assignment field, so the form finally reads the way your institution talks about its leads.

RELABELED  /  STOCK ODOO → LEAD CUSTOM LABELS
Expected Closing → Lead End Date
Contact / Partner tabs → Lead Info / Client Info
Company → Institute
— + Lead Assigned To (new field)
FOL. 02  —  WHAT'S INCLUDED
CRM label adjustments
"Expected Closing" becomes "Lead End Date" on the CRM form — a small change that keeps timeline tracking language consistent with how the team already talks about deadlines.
Custom tabs setup
Dedicated "Lead Info" and "Client Info" tabs replace the default Contact/Partner tabs, grouping assignment details and marketing fields the way your process actually reads them. See FIG. 01.
Resource management
A custom "Lead Assigned To" field sits right on the CRM form, so who's handling a lead is visible at a glance — no digging into activities or notes to find out.
Institute terminology
Partner records rename "Company" to "Institute," carrying the same relabeling through to contacts and client-facing records, not just the CRM pipeline.

FOL. 04  —  QUESTIONS
Does this change any CRM logic, or just the labels?
Just the view layer and one added field. Stages, pipelines, automations, and reporting underneath keep working exactly as they do in stock Odoo CRM.
Will "Institute" show up everywhere "Company" used to?
On Partner and CRM-facing views, yes — the relabeling follows the field wherever it's shown in those forms.
Can I keep the "Lead Assigned To" field even if I don't need the other relabeling?
The module ships as one package covering both the labels and the field, since both were built for the same institutional workflow they're intended to support together.
