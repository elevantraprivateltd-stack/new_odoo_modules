Module Overview

Ele Helpdesk is a complete standalone Helpdesk management system designed specifically for Odoo Community Edition. It allows businesses to manage customer support tickets, teams, stages, priorities, ticket types and departments from one centralized system. The module does not require the Odoo Enterprise Helpdesk application and provides its own ticket workflow, dashboards, customer portal and reporting features.

Key Features

Everything you need to manage your customer support operations.

Ticket Management

Create and manage customer support tickets with important information such as team, stage, priority, ticket type, department and source.

Kanban Workflow

Manage support tickets through an easy-to-use Kanban board with drag-and-drop stage management.

Helpdesk Teams

Organize customer support operations into different teams and assign tickets to the appropriate support team.

Custom Ticket Stages

Create and configure custom ticket stages according to your organization's support workflow.

Ticket Types

Categorize support requests using customizable ticket types for better organization and tracking.

Priority Management

Assign priorities to tickets and help support teams identify urgent customer requests quickly.

Custom Dashboard

Get a clear overview of helpdesk activities using Kanban, List, Pivot and Graph views.

Custom PDF Reports

Generate filterable PDF reports using the built-in Helpdesk report wizard and analyze support activities.

Customer Portal

Customers can access their support tickets through the Odoo portal and stay updated on their requests.

Website Support Form

Provide customers with an easy website contact form to submit new support requests directly to the Helpdesk system.

Community Edition Compatible

Ele Helpdesk is designed to work independently without the Enterprise Helpdesk application. It provides a complete Helpdesk workflow for businesses using Odoo Community Edition.

Simple Helpdesk Workflow

Manage customer requests from creation to resolution.

1. Create Ticket

Receive and create customer support requests.

2. Assign Team

Assign tickets to the appropriate support team.

3. Process Ticket

Track and update tickets through different stages.

4. Resolve

Complete and close customer support requests.
